Through the Beats

United by Rhythm

Welcome to our fansite dedicated to three exceptional drummers: Art Blakey, Jonathan Moffett, and Anika Nilles. Explore the site to discover their journey, their art, analyses of their performances, news and upcoming events. Join us in celebrating the rhythmic richness and global impact of these drumming masters!

Art Blakey

Pioneer of modern jazz, he revolutionized the art of drumming with his energy and improvisations. Mentor to generations of musicians and defender of authentic musical expression.

Jonathan Moffett

Iconic figure in pop and funk, he has accompanied the greatest artists like Michael Jackson. His percussive and spectacular style has marked the history of drumming on stage and in the studio.

Anika Nilles

Innovator of modern drumming, she fuses complex grooves and powerful melodies. Recognized for her virtuosity and creativity, she inspires a new generation of drummers around the world.
